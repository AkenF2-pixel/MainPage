Red circle = block signal. Blue square = path signal. The triangle shows the direction a signal faces. Signals sit on the right of travel. 1 cell = 100 m.

Scroll to zoom at the pointer. Signals and rails keep their screen size, so zoom in to spread crowded junctions apart. Right-drag or use the scrollbars to pan. Click two grid points to lay a rail. One-way rails run from the first point to the second. Bidirectional rails carry one train at a time. Path blocks must be level in game, and this planner is flat, so check slopes yourself.
